Is SkinSeek available now?

Yes. SkinSeek is live on the App Store for iPhone and on Google Play for Android. Download it, set up your skin profile and you can start scanning straight away.

How much does it cost?

Free to download and try. Premium features (unlimited scans, full dupe database, advanced routine builder) are £2.99 per month or £19.99 per year. No hidden costs.

Will it work for my skin tone?

Yes. SkinSeek's recommendations are calibrated for every Fitzpatrick skin tone. Pigmentation and SPF guidance changes based on your specific tone, not generic advice.

Yes. Your skin profile is stored securely and used only to personalise your SkinSeek experience. We never sell your data to third parties. UK GDPR-compliant.

Does it work for pregnancy?

Yes. SkinSeek automatically filters out ingredients that should be avoided during pregnancy and breastfeeding, including retinoids and high-concentration salicylic acid.

Will it have the products I use?

SkinSeek covers thousands of UK-available products including high street staples, pharmacy brands, and luxury skincare. If something's missing, OCR captures the ingredients from the bottle.
